# Onboarding checklist

SKILL-BODY-MARKER-ONBOARDING: cover every item below in your answer, in this order.

1. Call `kb_search` with a query about onboarding and read the matching article.
2. Contract: confirm the employment contract is signed and filed before the first day.
3. User account: the new employee needs a user account (email, VPN and HR portal access) created before the first day.
4. Equipment: the new employee needs a laptop and a headset ready on the first day.
5. Mentor: a mentor is assigned and the first-week meeting with them is scheduled.
6. Report back as a list with one line per item (contract, user account, equipment, mentor). Mark anything you cannot confirm as "still to be arranged" instead of saying it is done.

If `kb_search` fails or is unavailable, say so plainly and give the checklist from this procedure only, without quoting company documents you could not read.
